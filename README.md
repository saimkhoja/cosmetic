# QuickSales

Warehouse-to-till retail system for a cosmetics general dealer in Kinshasa, DR Congo:
one central store, several shops, real logins, a database in the cloud, and tills that keep selling without internet.

- **Web app**: React + TypeScript (`app/`), installable on the till PCs, served from a VPS.
- **Backend**: Supabase Cloud (PostgreSQL + Auth) with access rules and business logic in the database (`supabase/`).
- **Deploying**: see **[deploy/DEPLOY.md](deploy/DEPLOY.md)** (Supabase project setup, VPS with automatic HTTPS, first run, tills).
- The approved click-through demo is kept in [`demo/index.html`](demo/index.html) for presentations.

## Roles

There are no demo accounts. The first visit to a new installation shows a **setup screen** that creates the Admin; the Admin then creates every other login.

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| Admin | Everything: categories, carton cost (gross, and an optional net cost that counts instead), pc/half dozen/dozen/carton and set/piece prices, Excel import, delete or archive items, stock counts, deliveries, the books (suppliers, bills, payments, vouchers, statements, trial balance, profit and loss, balance sheet), upkeep, users (and the passcodes: a Shop Admin's for the Invoices tab and the shift end report, a till operator's for discounts), shops (add a shop; change a shop's name or address at any time, its code stays), settings, reports, activity log |
| Warehouse Operator | Item names, units and quantities, receive goods from a chosen supplier, review the shops' stock requests (approve, edit or reject), receive returns from the shops, see every shop's stock, send and correct deliveries, stock statements of the store and each shop. No cost prices per item, no price changes |
| Shop Admin | Approves (☑), rejects (❌) or edits the till operators' orders from the review panel, without touching the sale being made; approving takes the stock and completes the invoice the till operator printed (nothing prints unless the Shop Admin changed it). Also sells directly, gives goods on credit to vendeurs and takes their payments (when the Admin turned Vendeurs on), invoice discount (max 30%) as a percentage or an amount in CDF, the other worked out from the total, edits a confirmed invoice (quantities, units, discount) with a reason, deletes an invoice with a reason, duplicates, stock count sheet, orders stock from the central store, receives deliveries, sends returns to the store, price tickets, reports and the day end slip. The Invoices tab and printing the shift end report each need a passcode from the Admin; no Statements tab (the shop's ledgers are for the Admin) |
| Till Operator | Types the customer name, picks the items and sends the order for approval, which prints the invoice (customer and shop copies) for the customer to take to the Shop Admin; a discount on an invoice only with the Discount passcode from the Admin; price tickets. No cash, no invoice list, no reports |

## What it does

- **Search everything.** The Admin and the Shop Admin have a search box in the header (or press Ctrl+K or /). Type any part of a word or number and it lists every tab that holds it: items, invoices (searched on the server, however old), deliveries, stock requests, returns, and for the Admin also goods in, suppliers, ledgers, upkeep, users, shops and the activity log. Opening a result goes to that tab, scrolls to the record and highlights the word. The Inventory lists items with none in the store last, A to Z.
- **Gross and net cost.** Each item has a gross cost (of one carton, or of one set) and an optional net cost. When the Admin enters a net cost, it is the item's cost everywhere: the cost of goods sold, deliveries to the shops and goods in at cost, stock value at cost, margins, and the check that no selling price is below cost. Left empty, the gross cost counts. Items made before this keep their gross cost; changing or removing a net cost revalues the stock held, like any cost change, and is in the activity log. The Excel import takes it too (*Net carton cost USD*, optional).
- **Carton pricing.** An item is entered with the cost of one carton, the pcs in a carton and a selling price per pc, per half dozen, per dozen and per carton, each optional (at least one); the till offers only the units that have a price. An item with a carton price also sells by the **half carton** at half the carton price (rounded up to 50 CDF), when the carton holds an even number of pcs and only when that is a different number of pcs from its other units: with 24 to the carton the half carton is the dozen, so it is not offered twice. Stock is counted in pcs and shown as cartons + pcs. Mixed sets sell whole or one piece at a last price.
- **Categories** are made by the Admin. A seasonal category (school items, festive) has the months it sells; outside them its items go to the end of the lists.
- **Goods in, deliveries and returns to suppliers.** A scan box finds the item by any barcode or its code and puts the cursor in its quantity box with 1 filled in: type the count, press Enter and scan the next (a barcode scanned into a quantity box by mistake is caught). Rows stay where they are while quantities are typed; a search brings its matches to the top. Beside the list, *To receive* / *To send* / *To send back* lists only the lines entered, in order, in cartons and pcs, with warnings (more than the store has) and totals, to check before confirming.
- **Stock value at selling price.** On *Shop stock* the Admin sees each outlet's stock (central store and every shop) at selling price in CDF and USD, and at cost; choosing one shop adds the value of each item, and the stock count sheet window can print a stock valuation instead. The Shop Admin sees the shop's own total and the value of each item. Each pc is valued at its shop price as at the till (sets at the set price); stock sold on order counts as none. The warehouse sees quantities only.
- **Hot items first.** Inventory, shop stock and the till list the best sellers of the last 30 days first (marked Hot).
- **Delete items.** An item can be deleted once no stock is left anywhere; if it was ever received, sent or sold it is archived instead, so its history stays, and it can be restored.
- **Price tickets** for the shelves, in uniform bold capitals, on an A4 sheet (24 per page), a 50 x 30 mm label printer or the 80 mm receipt printer (one big bold 100 x 70 mm ticket per cut, printed sideways along the roll, the prices large, all the same size, in black boxes, or a small 70 x 35 mm one for items that take little shelf space), with the prices you tick: pcs, half dozen, dozen, carton or any mix.
- **Stock count sheet** for any shop or the central store: every item with the stock QuickSales shows and empty columns for the physical count.
- **Bulk import from Excel** (.xlsx or .csv, template included). Rows are checked first; existing names or codes are skipped; one bad row stops the import so nothing half-imports.
- **The store sends stock to the shops**, to one or more outlets at once (same quantities, one delivery note each). The Shop Admin checks what arrived and approves it, lowering a line if goods are short (with a note); only then does the stock leave the store and enter the shop. Goods already sent count as promised, so the same stock can't be sent twice. A delivery can be corrected or cancelled until it is received.
- **Orders approved by the Shop Admin.** The till operator starts each sale with the customer name, picks the items and sends the order. On the Shop Admin's till, 75% of the screen is the sale being made and 25% lists the orders waiting for review (hidden when till operators sell directly and nothing waits), each with its invoice number, the customer, the amount, ☑ approve, ❌ reject (with a reason the till operator sees) and Edit, which opens the order in its own window. A search box over that list finds a waiting order by customer, invoice number, till operator, an item on it or the amount, and the count shows how many of the orders match. Sending an order prints the **invoice** at the till operator's till, with that till's number (customer and shop copies), for the customer to take to the Shop Admin; no stock moves yet. Approving it as it is completes the sale and takes the stock, and nothing prints again: the invoices list holds that one invoice. If the Shop Admin changes the order first (quantities, units, items or a discount), the corrected invoice prints with the **same number**, marked FACTURE MODIFIÉE, the till operator's copy is void, and the invoice shows *Changed at approval* with the total the till printed. A rejected order's invoice number is void and never becomes an invoice. Orders sent from a till still running the old app (no invoice) are approved as before: the invoice prints at the Shop Admin's till.
- **The till screen.** The search box (and barcode scanner), Price check and Tickets sit in the top bar; the customer name above the items. The current sale lists each line in scan order with #, item, qty (− +), unit price, amount and ✕; the line just scanned or changed is highlighted and scrolled into view (↑ ↓ in the search box walk through the sale). **Park sale** puts a sale aside on the till (it survives a restart) so the next customer can be served; *Parked* brings it back. With *Enter the cash received* or *Accept payment in USD* on, **Take payment** opens the payment window (CDF and USD received, quick notes, change to give); otherwise *Take cash and print* sells at once.
- **Suppliers and goods in.** The Admin keeps each supplier's details, credit days, credit limit, opening balance and the items it supplies. A supplier can be deleted once nothing is owed either way; one with goods received, bills or payments is made inactive instead, keeping its history. The warehouse chooses the supplier and enters the quantities received (no prices); the Admin enters the supplier's bill later, which falls due after the credit days.
- **Stock requests from the shops.** The Shop Admin asks the central store for stock (Order stock). The warehouse sees each list next to what the store can send and the shop already has, then approves it as asked, edits the quantities (with a reason the shop sees) or rejects it. An approved request becomes a delivery that the Shop Admin receives as usual, so stock only moves when it arrives.
- **Returns to suppliers.** The warehouse sends goods back (damaged, expired, wrong item, not ordered) from Goods in: they leave the central store at once, a return note prints for the supplier's driver, and what is owed to the supplier drops at item cost. The Admin settles each return at the amount the supplier credits and sets it against a bill.
- **Goods billed but not received.** When the Admin enters the bill for a goods receipt, a Billed column per item records what the bill charges for; anything billed but not received becomes a claim against the supplier, set against that bill, so only what arrived is owed.
- **Returns to the store.** A shop sends goods back with a reason; they leave the shop at once and the warehouse receives what arrived (short goods are booked as a loss).
- **Book keeping like Tally.** Ledgers in groups (capital, loans, creditors, duties and taxes, fixed and current assets, cash, bank, stock, sales, direct and indirect income and expenses), vouchers (payment, receipt, contra, journal, debit and credit note) where each line is in USD or CDF with its rate, and bill-by-bill payments. QuickSales books the rest on its own: sales (cash, sales, VAT, cost of goods sold), deliveries to the shops at cost, goods in and bills (with the purchase price difference), returns, stock counts, cost changes and expenses. Statements of any ledger with or without the opening balance, day book, trial balance, profit and loss, balance sheet and money owed to suppliers by age. USD is the balancing currency.
- **Offline till.** Items, prices and shop stock are kept on the till. Without internet the Shop Admin keeps selling and printing and the till operator keeps preparing orders (they reach the Shop Admin when the connection returns); each till numbers its own invoices (`GOM-T1-000123`) so tills never clash, and sales are sent automatically when the connection returns. A sale sent twice is saved once.
- **Prices locked.** Only the Admin sets prices; shops sell exactly what was set. Invoices keep the prices and rate of the moment of sale.
- **Editable invoices** by the Shop Admin, with a reason: quantities, the unit of a line (for example pcs to dozen, charged at that unit's price) and the discount; stock is adjusted and the change is logged. An invoice can also be deleted with a reason: its stock goes back to the shop and it leaves sales, reports and the books, and the activity log keeps what it was.
- **Reports** over any dates, printable or as PDF. **Sales by category**: a pie of the categories' sales (after the invoice discounts; the 7 biggest, the rest as Other) beside the ranked list of every category the Admin made, with its amount and share (categories with no sales in the dates show as such); clicking a category lists the items sold in it, with how many of each unit, the pcs and the amount. A single day prints the **day end report** per shop in the shift end slip format: total invoices, invoice value in USD and CDF, collected, returned and balance.
- **A limit on the sales shown to the Shop Admin, and two passcodes.** The Shop Admin's figures stop at the shop's daily limit of 347 USD (815 450 CDF at a rate of 2 350): each day's sales are counted in the order they were made while they still fit under it, and the rest are left out of Sales today, the Sales report and the search. Everything on the screen agrees, so the figures cannot be added up past the limit, and the screen says the total stops there rather than passing it off as the day's takings. The sale is made, printed, taken from the stock and booked as usual, and the Admin sees every sale as before. The Shop Admin's **Invoices tab is locked**: it opens with the Invoices passcode the Admin gave, then lists every invoice of the shop, also those above the limit, to view, edit, delete or reprint as usual; it locks again on leaving the tab, with Lock, or after 30 minutes. **Printing the shift end (day end) report** asks for the Shift end report passcode; the printed slip has every sale of the day, so it matches the cash. The Admin sets, changes or removes both passcodes under **Users** (the shield button on a Shop Admin's row; 4 to 12 digits, kept only as hashes). Until a passcode is set, the tab or the report stays locked; five wrong tries block a passcode for 10 minutes; each use is in the activity log. Both need the connection. The Shop Admin has no Statements tab, where the ledgers list every sale. The limit is kept in the database (`settings.shop_sales_limit_usd`; empty means no limit).
- **A till operator's discount, with a passcode.** The till operator's till has a Discount button, locked. It opens with the Discount passcode the Admin gave that till operator (under **Users**, the shield button on their row), for one invoice per entry: a percentage or an amount in CDF, 30% at most. When orders go to the Shop Admin for approval, the invoice prints at the till with the discount, the Shop Admin sees the discount on the order, and approving it as printed prints nothing again; changing the discount prints the corrected invoice. When till operators sell directly, the sale is made with the discount. Without the passcode the server refuses a discount from a till operator. Typing the passcode needs the connection.
- **Shift end reports.** For shops that work in shifts. The Shop Admin prints today's shift end report from **Reports** (Today, then *Print day end report*, with the Shift end report passcode). A **Till** list chooses *All tills (combined)*, the default, or one till (Till T1, T2...). Printing closes the shift for that choice: the next report starts fresh with the sales made after it (shift 1, shift 2...), while Sales today, the sales report and the Admin's reports keep the whole day. Each till and the combined report have their own shifts. A shift holds the sales that reached the server since the last report, so a sale sent late from a till that was offline goes in the next shift instead of being missed; an invoice correction counts in the shift it was made in. The *Report* list can print a shift again, the same as it was, marked as a copy. From the second shift of the day, the slip ends with one more line, **Total of the day**: the invoice value of today's shift slips added up, so it agrees with the slips already printed. The first shift has no such line, as it would only repeat itself. A past day prints the whole day for all tills or one till, and closes nothing.
- **Vendeurs: goods on credit from the shops.** A switch in **Settings → Options → Selling**, off by default. With it on, the Shop Admin has a **Vendeurs** tab, like the Admin's suppliers but the other way round: each vendeur (a reseller) has a name, phone, a **credit period** in days, a **credit limit** in CDF and, if any, what they already owed. At the till the Shop Admin adds the goods and presses **On credit**: they choose the vendeur, the invoice prints in the vendeur's name marked *Vente à crédit* with its due date, the stock leaves the shop, and nothing is paid at the till. Goods can still be given when the vendeur is **overdue** or would go **over the limit**: the till shows a reminder first, the Vendeurs tab flags them (overdue days and amount, over the limit by how much), and the activity log notes it. **Payments**, partial or full, are taken in the Vendeurs tab in Francs, dollars or both (with a receipt for the vendeur). Dollars count at the internal rate, shown already filled in; the Shop Admin can type the taux agreed with the vendeur instead (a rate far from the internal one, such as a zero missing, is refused), and the statement, receipt and activity log say which rate was used. Dollars worth more than is owed get the rest back in Francs. Dollars go into the shop's USD cash and count in Collected USD on the shift end report: what the vendeur owes goes down, so the credit available comes back; payments settle the oldest goods first. A payment taken by mistake can be cancelled with a reason. A vendeur created by mistake is **deleted** with the bin button on its row (or in its edit window), even if an amount owed was typed; one with goods, payments or promo items is never deleted but made inactive once it owes nothing, keeping its history. Each vendeur has a **statement** (goods, payments, running balance, printable) and a ledger under Sundry Debtors in the Admin's books. **Promo items** are given free, with goods on credit or on their own: picked from the shop's stock, not on the invoice and not owed, valued at the shop price, and booked at cost as *Promotions (goods given free)*; an unsettled promo can be cancelled, its items going back into the stock. Each month the **promo report** lists each item given out as promo and how many pieces, all vendeurs together (no vendeur names); the Vendeurs tab says when last month's is ready. **Keep report** saves it and its promos stay open (they keep adding up into the next month); **Settle** closes them, the promo count starts again at zero, and the settled report stays to view and print. The shift end report and the day end slip count cash only: goods on credit show as *On credit* and are left out of Collected, and vendeurs' payments are added to it (in the all-tills report).
- **Shops without a central store.** A switch in **Settings → Options → Stock**, off by default. With it on the business keeps no warehouse: **Receive from supplier** asks which shop the goods go into, the quantities are added to that shop's stock and booked to that shop's stock account, and each receipt remembers its shop. The tabs that only move stock between the store and the shops (Deliveries and Stock requests) are hidden for every role, and Inventory and the dashboard count what the shops hold together instead of the store. **Add stock to shops** on Shop stock (the Admin) puts stock straight into the shops ticked, by typing quantities or with **Import from Excel** in the item template (gross and net carton cost, prices, cartons and pcs to add): items already in the catalogue only get the quantity, items not in it are made from the file with its details, and one bad row stops the import so nothing half-imports. The shop chosen on the page comes ticked; the stock is booked at cost as opening stock. The buttons that send stock from the central store are hidden. **Returns** stays for the Shop Admin and the Admin: the Shop Admin sends goods back as before (they leave the shop at once and can be cancelled while they wait); the Admin presses **Decide**, counts what came back (anything missing is booked as a loss, with a note) and chooses where it goes: **back to the supplier** (a return to supplier is made, what is owed drops at item cost, a return note can be printed, and it is settled or cancelled under Accounts; cancelling puts the goods back in the shop that sent them), **to another shop** (added to that shop's stock), or **thrown away** (booked as a stock adjustment). The Shop Admin sees what was decided. When the Admin adds stock to a shop, or receives goods from a supplier straight into it, the shop's **Shop Admin is told**: a notice slides in at the top right (not over the till while selling) with the number of items and units; opening it lists each item, the quantity received (cartons and pcs), its selling prices and the value at selling price, and **Got it** marks it seen. **Stock received** on the Stock page shows what arrived before. Turning it off puts everything back: whatever is already in the central store stays there. Turning it off puts everything back: whatever is already in the central store stays there.
- **Supermarket switches** (Settings → Till options, all off by default, in groups that open and close: Selling, Barcodes, Payment, Printing; each group shows how many of its switches are on). **Customer name optional**: walk-in sales and orders without a name. **Barcode scanning**: items carry one or more barcodes, up to 60 ("+ Add another barcode" in the item form, or several in one Excel cell separated with ;); scanning any of them at the till adds 1 pc to the sale, and price check finds them too. **Till operators sell directly**: no shop admin approval; the till operator takes the cash and prints the invoice (a discount only with the Discount passcode). **Enter the cash received**: the cashier types the amount given; the till shows the change and the receipt prints the cash received and the change. **Accept payment in USD**: customers pay in dollars, Francs or both, the dollars valued at the internal rate; change in CDF or whole dollars plus CDF; dollars are kept in each shop's Cash USD ledger and shown on the day end slip. **Print one copy of each invoice**: every till prints the customer copy only instead of the customer and shop copies; each invoice remembers how many copies it printed with, so Invoices still counts duplicates right.
- **Light or dark screens.** The button next to Sign out switches between Auto (follows the device), Light and Dark; each computer or phone remembers its choice.
- **Security.** Real passwords (hashed by Supabase Auth; 10+ characters with letters and numbers), each person chooses their own at first sign-in, disabled accounts are shut out at once, the screen locks after 15 minutes, access rules on every table, every change goes through checked database functions, and a full activity log.

## Currency

All cost and selling prices are stored in USD. Francs are shown as CDF everywhere. There are two rates, each editable at any time:

- **Sales rate (taux de vente)**, in Settings or with the *Sales rate* button on the Items page: the shop price in Francs is USD × this rate, rounded up to the next 50 CDF (4 220 → 4 250, 4 260 → 4 300; the screens show the Francs added as "+N rounded"), so changing it reprices every shop, ticket and till at once. Each invoice keeps its Franc prices and the rate of its sale, so past invoices never change, and the sales it collects are booked at that rate.
- **Money exchange at the counter.** A switch in **Settings → Options → Payment**, off by default, with a **buying rate** (what the shop pays for 1 USD) and a **selling rate** (what it asks; not below the buying rate). The Shop Admin's **Exchange** tab records money changed for people who come to the shop, out of the shop's cash: the customer gives dollars and gets Francs (rounded down to 50 FC), or gives Francs (rounded up to 50 FC) and gets dollars. The rate is filled in and can be changed for one exchange; a receipt can be printed. The books move the money between the shop's Cash and Cash USD at the internal rate, and the difference (the shop's margin) goes to Exchange gain on dollars, as a gain or a loss. The Shop Admin or the Admin cancels one with a reason; the Admin sees every shop's exchanges.
- **What the shift end report counts.** Besides the sales, and only when there is any (a business without vendeurs, exchanges or such expenses gets the same slip as before): the cash sales apart from the vendeurs, each vendeur who paid by name in USD and CDF, the dollars and Francs exchanged, and the expenses the Admin paid from the shop's cash (Store upkeep, *Paid from* Cash – shop or Cash USD – shop, in the currency that cash holds), which are taken off the balance. The balance is what should be in the drawer. Expenses count in the report of all tills: by the day of the expense on the day end slip, and by when they were recorded on a shift end report.
- **Daily cash report of a shop (Admin).** On Reports, choose a shop and the day (or dates), then **Print cash report**: every move of the shop's two cash drawers from the books, CDF and USD side by side, with In, Out and the running Balance: cash sales (one line a day), each vendeur's payment (credit collections), each money exchange, each expense paid from the shop's cash with its type, and other entries such as cash taken to head office (a contra voucher from the shop's cash). Several days get a total each; the summary at the end gives the cash collected, the exchanges, the expenses, the other moves and the cash in hand.
- **Store upkeep (expenses).** The Admin records running costs in USD or Francs, paid from a cash or bank ledger; each is booked as a payment voucher. Types: Rent, Electricity & fuel, Security, Salaries, Local staff salary, Overtime, Journalier (labour), Transport, Local transport, Moto expense, Maintenance, Stationery, Food expense, Taxes & licences, Miscellaneous expense and Other, each with its own ledger under Indirect expenses. The pencil on a row **corrects** an expense (date, type, description, amount, currency, paid from): the same record and the same voucher number are updated, at the rate it was first booked at, and the activity log keeps what it was before. An expense whose voucher was cancelled under Accounts is recorded again instead. The bin **deletes** an expense, with a reason: its payment voucher is cancelled (the money goes back to the cash or bank it was paid from) and the activity log keeps what it was.
- **Internal rate (taux interne)**, in Settings: the default for supplier bills and payments, expenses (upkeep), vouchers such as cash taken from the shops, and opening balances in Francs. Any entry can still be given its own rate. It is also **what a dollar is worth at the till**: set it to what the shop gives for a dollar over the counter. When it is below the sales rate, a customer paying in dollars owes the difference in Francs, the till asks for it before the sale can be finished, and it shows on the shift end report as Francs collected. Each invoice keeps the dollar rate of its moment, and what the shop earns on the exchange is booked to *Exchange gain on dollars*. Sales are counted in dollars at that same rate, so the shift end report's *Invoice Value USD* is the dollars the sales are worth over the counter and ties with *Collected USD*, and the Admin's sales in dollars match the cash. The Francs are untouched; gross profit stays on the books' basis, which values a sale at the sales rate.

Payments at the till are in Francs only. Reports convert Francs back to USD with each invoice's own rate.

```
  src/pages/          screens, one file per area

```

## Development

```bash
supabase/local/start.sh            # local database, Auth, REST and functions on http://127.0.0.1:54321 (needs Docker, Deno, psql)
cd app && npm install
cp .env.example .env.local         # URL http://127.0.0.1:54321 and the ANON_KEY printed by start.sh
npm run dev                        # http://localhost:5173
```

Checks: `supabase/tests/run.sh` (database), `npm test` and `npx tsc -b` (app), `npx playwright test` (end to end, against a fresh local stack and `vite preview` on port 4173).
